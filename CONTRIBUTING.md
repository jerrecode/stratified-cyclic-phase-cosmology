# Contributing

Scientific changes must identify whether they alter ontology, action, equations of motion, numerical method, observable mapping, likelihood, data selection, or presentation only.

Every physical-model change should include:

1. assumptions and degrees of freedom;
2. units and parameter dimensions;
3. variational or explicitly declared effective-equation derivation;
4. conservation and constraint identities;
5. a GR or benchmark limiting case;
6. ghost, gradient, and strong-coupling conditions where applicable;
7. analytical, manufactured-solution, or parameter-recovery tests;
8. tolerance and resolution convergence evidence;
9. predeclared falsification criteria;
10. regenerated manuscript assets and provenance where applicable.

Do not infer empirical support from visual resemblance. Do not identify finite differences, a thermodynamic Hessian, or field-space curvature with spacetime curvature without a derivation. Large downloaded products and generated results must not be committed; register their immutable identifiers, checksums, access parameters, and transformations instead.
