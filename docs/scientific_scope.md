# Scientific scope and nonclaims

## Baseline ontology

The baseline theory contains a Lorentzian metric, ordinary matter and radiation, and a canonical scalar stratification field. Distinguishable phase strata are minima or sectors of the field potential. Their integer labels are not assumed to be physical discrete time.

## Structures kept distinct

- **Spacetime curvature:** Ricci and Riemann quantities derived from the metric.
- **Field-space topology or geometry:** properties of the stratification-field target space.
- **Thermodynamic state-space geometry:** a fluctuation metric derived from a declared ensemble and thermodynamic potential.
- **Numerical grid:** an approximation whose artifacts must vanish under convergence.

## Baseline nonclaims

The bootstrap does not claim a proven stable cosmological limit cycle, fundamental discrete time, a physical vortex without an invariant, an entropy reset, a CMB or gravitational-wave detection, or validity beyond the declared effective theory.

## Rejection criteria

A candidate realization is rejected when it violates declared constraint tolerances, develops non-finite or physically invalid states, lacks a controlled standard-cosmology limit, or contains a ghost, gradient instability, or unresolved strong-coupling regime. A spectral feature is rejected if it is unstable against sampling, duration, window, solver, resolution, prior, or look-elsewhere corrections. A topological claim is rejected unless an invariant distinguishes the proposed sectors or defects.
