\section{Conclusion}
The audited \SCPC{} repository now has a reproducible mathematical and computational baseline with machine-enforced evidential dependencies. The covariant equations, null-energy-condition restriction, exact turning-point threshold, sparse-redshift distance calculation, full-covariance BAO machinery, topology classification, and fixed-$S^4$ finite-volume test field are derived or independently checked in code. Exact root-localized $H=0$ events are now additionally authenticated by an independent local physics audit, and a Stage~1 recurrence candidate must reproduce its complete event topology under tighter tolerances and an algorithmically distinct solver before any stability calculation is permitted.

For the configured canonical expanding background, the negative theoretical result is now analytic rather than merely finite-time numerical. Its positive potential floor $V_{\min}=3.5\Mpl^4$ implies $a_\star\le\sqrt{3/3.5}=0.9258\ldots$ at every regular turning point, while the trajectory begins at $a_0=1$ with $H_0>0$. The baseline therefore cannot undergo a future regular turnaround and cannot become cyclic at any later integration time. Numerical convergence confirms the implementation of that trajectory; extending the same integration horizon cannot reverse the theorem. This falsifies recurrence for the configured baseline but does not prejudge other admissible parameter regions or modified actions.

The DESI DR2 calculation strengthens the observational side of the project without changing that theoretical conclusion. The official released 2025 BAO and BAO+BBN chains are processed with convergence diagnostics and GetDist, the calibrated background is computed with CAMB, and the reproduced flat-$\Lambda$CDM constraints agree with the published DESI benchmark. The 2026 DESI Lyman-$\alpha$ full-shape/AP result is tracked separately, including the validation-driven exclusion of its biased $f\sigma_8$ estimator, and is not combined with the pinned 2025 covariance in the absence of a release-native joint likelihood. These steps validate and future-proof the inference infrastructure; they are not a calibrated \SCPC{} fit.

No nonsingular recurrent \SCPC{} solution, perturbatively stable cycle, calibrated \SCPC{} BAO prediction, or self-gravitating $4+1$-dimensional layered universe has yet been demonstrated. The immediate scientific target is therefore narrower and more decisive: search the analytically admissible Stage~1 domain and either produce a repeated-return background that passes the complete candidate gate or accumulate a converged exclusion of that domain. Only the former outcome unlocks Poincar\'e/Floquet analysis; only a stable background then unlocks perturbations and observable inference. Keeping numerical consistency, local turning physics, recurrence, stability, geometric analogy, and observational agreement distinct is a central result of the audit.
