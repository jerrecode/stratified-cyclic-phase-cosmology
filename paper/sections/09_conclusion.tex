\section{Conclusion}
The audited \SCPC{} repository now has a reproducible mathematical and computational baseline. The covariant equations, null-energy-condition restriction, exact turning-point threshold, sparse-redshift distance calculation, full-covariance BAO machinery, topology classification, and fixed-$S^4$ finite-volume test field are derived or independently checked in code. The configured canonical background is well resolved but monotonic on the declared interval: it contains no bounce or turnaround. This negative result is not repaired or obscured by parameter fitting.

The DESI DR2 calculation strengthens the observational side of the project without changing that theoretical conclusion. The official released BAO and BAO+BBN chains are processed with convergence diagnostics and GetDist, the calibrated background is computed with CAMB, and the reproduced flat-$\Lambda$CDM constraints agree with the published DESI benchmark. That agreement validates the likelihood and inference infrastructure; it is not a calibrated \SCPC{} fit.

No nonsingular recurrent \SCPC{} solution, perturbatively stable cycle, calibrated \SCPC{} BAO prediction, or self-gravitating $4+1$-dimensional layered universe has yet been demonstrated. The present contribution instead identifies exact analytical conditions, reproducible numerical gates, observational validation tests, and missing definitions that any stronger claim must satisfy. Keeping numerical consistency, geometric analogy, recurrence, perturbative stability, and observational agreement distinct is itself a necessary scientific result.
