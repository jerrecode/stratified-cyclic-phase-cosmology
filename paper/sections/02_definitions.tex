\section{Definitions and scope}
\label{sec:definitions}

A \emph{phase stratum} is a connected or metastable sector of the stratification-field state space. It is not automatically a discrete instant of physical time. The baseline spacetime is a smooth Lorentzian manifold $(\mathcal{M},g_{\mu\nu})$. A \emph{cosmological cycle} is a recurrent trajectory in the reduced dynamical state space containing both a bounce and a turnaround. A \emph{vortex} will be claimed only when a nonzero vorticity tensor, circulation, winding number, or related topological invariant is explicitly constructed.

Spacetime Ricci curvature $R[g]$, field-space curvature $\Rfield$, thermodynamic state-space curvature $\Rth$, and numerical finite differences are distinct quantities. They must not be identified by notation or analogy alone.
