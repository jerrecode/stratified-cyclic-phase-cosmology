\section{Definitions and assumptions}
We use a continuous Lorentzian spacetime $(\mathcal M,g_{\mu\nu})$ with signature $(-,+,+,+)$. The stratification field $\phi$ is a physical scalar order parameter. Labels of potential minima or basins are phase-stratum labels, not fundamental time coordinates and not separate universes. Spacetime curvature, field-space curvature, thermodynamic state-space curvature, embedding curvature, and finite-difference operators are treated as distinct objects.

A regular bounce satisfies $H=0$, $\dot H>0$, $0<a<\infty$, finite matter variables, and finite curvature invariants. A turnaround satisfies the same regularity conditions with $\dot H<0$. A cosmological cycle requires both event classes and recurrence in the full constrained dynamical state, not merely an oscillatory projection. A stable limit cycle additionally requires stability of the Poincar\'e return map; repeated zero crossings of $H$ are insufficient.

The baseline scalar target is $\mathbb R$. Although its potential is periodic, $\phi$ and $\phi+2\pi f/N_s$ are not identified. A compact target $S^1$ is a different model and must be declared explicitly. Likewise, ``layer'' has two uses that must not be conflated: a phase stratum is a basin in field space, whereas an $S^3$ leaf at fixed higher-dimensional polar coordinate is a spatial hypersurface.
