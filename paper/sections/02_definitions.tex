\section{Definitions and assumptions}
We use a continuous Lorentzian spacetime $(\mathcal M,g_{\mu\nu})$. The stratification field $\phi$ is a physical scalar order parameter; labels of potential minima or basins are phase-stratum labels rather than fundamental time coordinates. Spacetime curvature, field-space curvature, thermodynamic state-space curvature, and finite-difference operators are treated as distinct objects.

A bounce satisfies $H=0$, $\dot H>0$, and $a>0$. A turnaround satisfies $H=0$ and $\dot H<0$. A cosmological cycle requires both events and recurrence in the full dynamical state, not merely an oscillatory projection.
