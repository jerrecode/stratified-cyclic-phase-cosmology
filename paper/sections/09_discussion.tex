\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The stratified-field interpretation preserves the motivating notion of distinguishable cosmological sectors without presupposing a preferred discrete time coordinate. The canonical closed-FLRW system is deliberately minimal: it establishes a transparent reference against which more general scalar-tensor, effective quantum-cosmological, or thermodynamic extensions can be evaluated. Failure of the baseline to generate robust cycles would be scientifically informative and would constrain the type of additional mechanism required.

Information geometry may later be used as a diagnostic only after specifying a thermodynamic ensemble and a convex potential. Relations between gravitation and thermodynamics require defined heat fluxes, temperatures, horizons, and entropy currents rather than formal symbol substitution \citep{jacobson1995thermodynamics,ruppeiner1979thermodynamics}.
