\section{Observational data and comparison models}
The public-data registry is machine validated and records release versions, access protocols,
product roles, variables, units, dimensions, and retrieval instructions. The initial core background
inputs are Planck CMB likelihood products, DESI BAO measurements, and Type Ia supernova distance
data. ACT, DES, eBOSS, Euclid, GWOSC, and NANOGrav products support later perturbation, growth,
map, and gravitational-wave tests.

For the DESI DR2 calibration test we pin the 13-element Results I/II Gaussian BAO vector and its
full covariance at the upstream \texttt{bao\_data} commit recorded in the inference configuration.
The authoritative row order mixes $D_M/r_d$, $D_H/r_d$, and $D_V/r_d$ measurements. Distances are
integrated from the physical lower limit $z=0$ rather than from the first requested data redshift.
DESI DR1 and DR2 are overlapping releases and are never multiplied as statistically independent
likelihoods.

BAO alone constrains the ruler combination $h\,r_d$ together with late-time shape parameters; it
does not separately measure $H_0$ and $r_d$. The BBN calibration enters as a prior on the physical
baryon density, $\omega_b=\Omega_bh^2$, and $r_d$ is then calculated under the declared standard
early-Universe assumptions. It is therefore misleading to draw BBN as though it were a direct
measurement band in the $(H_0,r_d)$ plane.

Publication posterior plots use the official DESI DR2 Cobaya MCMC chains released for Results II,
not inverse-Jacobian errors from a local optimizer. The deterministic repository optimizer is kept
only as a regression check of the pinned Gaussian likelihood. High-redshift background quantities
and the drag horizon use CAMB, including the redshift-dependent massive-neutrino contribution.
The later 2026 DESI Lyman-$\alpha$ AP+BAO update is tracked separately and is not inserted into the
pinned 2025 covariance without a release-native joint likelihood or covariance.

Standard comparison curves are generated on identical redshift grids for flat $\Lambda$CDM,
curved $\Lambda$CDM, $w$CDM, and CPL models. Literature cyclic models will be included only through
validated reproduction adapters rather than hand-entered curves.
