\section{Observational data and comparison models}
The public-data registry is machine validated and records release versions, access protocols, product roles, variables, units, dimensions, and retrieval instructions. The initial core background inputs are Planck CMB likelihood products, DESI BAO measurements, and Type Ia supernova distance data. ACT, DES, eBOSS, Euclid, GWOSC, and NANOGrav products support later perturbation, growth, map, and gravitational-wave tests.

Standard comparison curves are generated on identical redshift grids for flat $\Lambda$CDM, curved $\Lambda$CDM, $w$CDM, and CPL models. Literature cyclic models will be included only through validated reproduction adapters rather than hand-entered curves.
