\section{Homogeneous cosmology}
For
\begin{equation}
ds^2=-dt^2+a^2(t)d\Sigma_k^2,
\end{equation}
the equations in reduced Planck units are
\begin{align}
H^2+\frac{k}{a^2}&=\frac{1}{3}(\rho_m+\rho_r+\rho_\phi),\\
\dot H&=\frac{k}{a^2}-\frac12\left(\rho_m+\frac43\rho_r+\dot\phi^2\right),\\
\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V_{,\phi}&=0,
\end{align}
with $\rho_\phi=\dot\phi^2/2+V$ and $p_\phi=\dot\phi^2/2-V$. These equations define the first computational test of the framework.
