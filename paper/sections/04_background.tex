\section{Homogeneous cosmology}
For
\begin{equation}
ds^2=-dt^2+a^2(t)d\Sigma_k^2,
\end{equation}
the independent homogeneous equations are
\begin{align}
H^2+\frac{k}{a^2}&=\frac{1}{3\Mpl^2}(\rho_m+\rho_r+\rho_\phi),\\
\dot H&=\frac{k}{a^2}-\frac{1}{2\Mpl^2}\left(\rho_m+\frac43\rho_r+\dot\phi^2\right),\\
\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V_{,\phi}&=0,
\end{align}
with
\begin{equation}
\rho_\phi=\frac12\dot\phi^2+V,\qquad
p_\phi=\frac12\dot\phi^2-V,\qquad
\rho_m\propto a^{-3},\qquad \rho_r\propto a^{-4}.
\end{equation}
The acceleration equation follows from these equations and conservation,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{\rho+3p}{6\Mpl^2}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Exact turning-point conditions}
At a regular turning point $H_\star=0$, the Friedmann constraint requires
\begin{equation}
\rho_\star=\frac{3\Mpl^2k}{a_\star^2}.
\end{equation}
For positive total density this immediately excludes a regular turning point for $k\le0$. Eliminating the curvature term from the Raychaudhuri equation gives the exact identity
\begin{align}
\dot H_\star
&=-\frac{\rho_\star+3p_\star}{6\Mpl^2}\\
&=\left.\frac{2V-\rho_m-2\rho_r-2\dot\phi^2}{6\Mpl^2}\right|_\star.
\end{align}
Thus the canonical dust--radiation--scalar system bounces if and only if
\begin{equation}
V(\phi_\star)>\frac12\rho_m(a_\star)+\rho_r(a_\star)+\dot\phi_\star^2,
\end{equation}
subject to the Friedmann constraint and regularity. Reversing the inequality gives a turnaround. Equality is a degenerate stationary point for which higher derivatives must be examined. These conditions are implemented as analytical filters and tests; they do not imply that a globally recurrent orbit exists.

\subsection{What remains free}
Initial data contain $(a,\phi,\dot\phi)$ plus the sign of $H$, with the magnitude of $H$ fixed by the constraint. A time origin and the overall normalization of $a$ are coordinate conventions. In contrast, the potential parameters and present component densities are physical inputs. They cannot all be auto-derived from the background equations because the system would otherwise be underdetermined. They may be fitted only after a calibrated mapping from the natural-unit solution to measured redshift, the sound horizon, and perturbation observables has been derived. Selecting them merely to force a stable cycle would be circular.
