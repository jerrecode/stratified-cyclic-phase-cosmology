\section{Covariant baseline theory}
The baseline action is
\begin{equation}
S=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{\Mpl^2}{2}R-\frac12 g^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla_\nu\phi-V(\phi)\right]+S_m+S_r.
\end{equation}
A periodic stratification potential is parameterized as
\begin{equation}
V(\phi)=V_{\rm off}+V_0\left[1-\cos\left(\frac{N_s\phi}{f}\right)\right].
\end{equation}
Variation gives Einstein's equation with ordinary matter, radiation, and the scalar stress-energy tensor. The canonical baseline has a controlled GR limit and no higher-derivative degrees of freedom. It is deliberately minimal and may fail to generate a viable cycle.
