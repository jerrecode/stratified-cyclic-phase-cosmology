\section{Results protocol}
\label{sec:results}

Results will be reported in a hierarchy. First, analytical limiting cases and numerical convergence are established. Second, background trajectories are classified into singular, monotonic, single-bounce, transiently oscillatory, recurrent, or stable cyclic classes. Third, perturbative kinetic and gradient coefficients are evaluated. Only models surviving these stages proceed to primordial spectra, Boltzmann observables, and parameter inference.

The present repository bootstrap contains the computational protocol and verification baseline. Quantitative claims about viable cycles or data preference are intentionally deferred until the corresponding tests and release-tagged result artifacts exist.
