\begin{abstract}
We audit and extend Stratified Cyclic Phase Cosmology (\SCPC), a research framework in which a scalar order parameter labels phase strata while cyclicity remains a hypothesis to be tested. Starting from the Einstein--Hilbert action plus a canonical scalar, dust, and radiation, we derive the homogeneous equations and an exact turning-point criterion. At any regular turning point,
$\dot H=-(\rho+3p)/(6\Mpl^2)$; hence the baseline can bounce only if
$V>\rho_m/2+\rho_r+\dot\phi^2$, and a positive-density turning point requires positive spatial curvature. The configured baseline is numerically consistent but not cyclic: over $0\le t\le8\,\Mpl^{-1}$ it contains no root-localized turning point and reaches a maximum normalized Friedmann residual of $8.75\times10^{-11}$.

We validate the observational machinery against the pinned DESI DR2 2025 BAO likelihood without combining the overlapping DR1 and DR2 releases. BAO alone identifies the ruler combination $h r_d$ rather than independent $H_0$ and $r_d$. The official released DESI MCMC chains, analysed with GetDist and CAMB under the stated BBN calibration, reproduce the published flat-$\Lambda$CDM constraints; the repository optimizer is retained only as an independent full-covariance regression check. Finally, we distinguish a cyclic Lorentzian $S^3$ history from an $S^4$ spatial foliation and implement an $S^3$-invariant scalar test field on fixed $\mathbb R\times S^4$ with a finite-volume energy diagnostic matched to the discrete Laplacian. Gravitational backreaction is deliberately absent. These results establish reproducible constraints, validation tests, and failure modes; they do not establish a nonsingular recurrent universe or a self-gravitating five-dimensional solution.
\end{abstract}
