\begin{abstract}
We introduce Stratified Cyclic Phase Cosmology (\SCPC), a covariant research framework in which a dynamical stratification field may organize cosmological states into distinguishable phase strata. We formulate a conservative canonical scalar-field baseline, derive its homogeneous FLRW equations, define operational bounce and turnaround criteria, and implement constraint-monitored numerical evolution. The initial study is designed to determine whether nonsingular recurrent solutions exist and remain stable; cyclicity is tested rather than assumed. A reproducible public-data manifest and standard-model comparison pipeline establish the route from theory to falsifiable background, perturbation, and observational predictions.
\end{abstract}
