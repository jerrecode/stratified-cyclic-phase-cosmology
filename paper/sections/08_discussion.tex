\section{Discussion and falsification criteria}
At a high level, the project separates four questions: whether the action is coherent, whether a recurrent background exists, whether perturbations are healthy, and whether derived observables fit data. At a low level, each gate corresponds to a checkable artifact: conservation and constraint residuals with root-localized events; Poincar\'e maps and Floquet multipliers; quadratic kinetic and gradient matrices; and release-native likelihoods with transfer functions and nuisance parameters.

The present canonical baseline passes the numerical-consistency gate for the configured trajectory but does not exhibit a turning point. Its positive vacuum offset drives monotonic, approximately de Sitter expansion, while the exact bounce condition requires closed spatial curvature plus sufficiently strong potential domination. No parameter fit can repair a failure of the declared action or prove recurrence from a finite trajectory. Conversely, a negative result in a declared converged parameter domain would falsify this baseline without falsifying every possible cyclic theory.

The DESI result has a similarly bounded interpretation. Reproducing the released flat-$\Lambda$CDM chain constraints demonstrates that the BAO vector, covariance ordering, ruler calibration, GetDist chain handling, and CAMB background machinery are functioning consistently. It does not test \SCPC{} until a calibrated \SCPC{} $H(z)$, $r_d$, and ultimately perturbation transfer functions have been derived. The distinction between comparator validation and theory inference is maintained in figure captions, generated metadata, and the measurement-interface table.

The next defensible calculations are therefore:
\begin{enumerate}
\item scan the closed-FLRW parameter domain using the exact turning-point condition as an analytical prefilter, while preserving failed and domain-terminated runs;
\item for candidates with repeated root-localized events, compute a Poincar\'e map, monodromy matrix, Floquet multipliers, basin size, and tolerance/solver convergence;
\item derive the quadratic scalar and tensor perturbation actions and reject ghost, gradient-unstable, or strongly coupled regions;
\item only after those gates connect the background and perturbations to CLASS, CAMB, or another validated Boltzmann solver \cite{class2011,camb2000};
\item perform synthetic parameter recovery before attempting joint Planck/ACT, DESI, supernova, growth, lensing, or gravitational-wave inference.
\end{enumerate}

The canonical model is rejected as a cyclic theory if no stable nonsingular cycles occur in a declared, converged domain. A proposed extension is rejected if it violates stress-energy conservation, lacks a controlled standard-model limit, contains a ghost or unacceptable gradient instability, or places its strong-coupling scale below the calculation. A spectral feature is accepted as physical only if it survives resolution, duration, cadence, window, transfer-function, foreground, and look-elsewhere tests. Repeated close returns in a single integration are not, by themselves, evidence for a stable limit cycle.
