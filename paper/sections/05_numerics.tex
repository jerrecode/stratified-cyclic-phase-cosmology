\section{Numerical implementation}
The background system is integrated with adaptive explicit Runge--Kutta methods and continuous event detection at $H=0$. The Friedmann constraint is monitored through the normalized residual
\begin{equation}
\epsilon_F=\frac{H^2+k/a^2-\rho_{\rm tot}/3}{\max(|\rho_{\rm tot}/3|,\epsilon_*)}.
\end{equation}
Publication results require tolerance convergence, solver cross-checks, and a declared maximum residual. Software and figures are generated from the same serialized arrays in accordance with reproducible-research practice \cite{wilkinson2016,barker2022}.
