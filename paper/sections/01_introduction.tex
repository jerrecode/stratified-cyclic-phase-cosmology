\section{Introduction}
The standard cosmological model provides an exceptionally successful description of a wide range of observations, while leaving open questions concerning initial conditions, singularities, vacuum energy, and the global structure of cosmological history \cite{planck2018,desi2025}. Cyclic and bouncing cosmologies investigate whether the hot expanding phase can be embedded in a nonsingular recurrent history \cite{ashtekar2011,lehners2008}.

The purpose of \SCPC{} is not to infer cyclicity from suggestive numerical patterns. It is to define a model hierarchy in which each proposed mechanism is covariant, dimensionally explicit, numerically verifiable, perturbatively stable, and confrontable with public likelihoods.
