\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Precision observations strongly constrain departures from the standard cosmological model, but foundational questions concerning initial conditions, singularities, entropy, and the global history of spacetime remain open. Cyclic and bouncing proposals replace a singular beginning with one or more transitions between contracting and expanding regimes, but such constructions face stringent requirements from covariance, stress-energy conservation, perturbative stability, and cosmological data \citep{lehners2008ekpyrotic,ashtekar2011lqc}.

The purpose of \SCPC\ is not to infer a theory from a visual metaphor. It is to formulate a hierarchy of explicit models in which distinguishable phase strata have a mathematical definition, cyclicity is a property to be demonstrated, and every proposed observational signature follows from a deterministic parameter-to-observable map. This paper establishes terminology, the baseline action, numerical standards, public-data interfaces, and falsification criteria.
