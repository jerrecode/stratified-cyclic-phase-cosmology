\section{Computational results}
\subsection{Canonical background: converged and analytically excluded from future recurrence}
The configured initial state satisfies the closed-FLRW constraint and selects the expanding branch. Over $0\le t\le8\,\Mpl^{-1}$, the scale factor grows from $1$ to $3.949\times10^3$, $H$ grows from $0.426$ to $1.080$ in natural units, and the scalar relaxes toward the potential minimum. No root-localized bounce or turnaround is detected. The maximum normalized Friedmann residual is $8.75\times10^{-11}$; the stored medium-tolerance solution differs from the reference by at most $4.66\times10^{-8}$, and the RK45 solution differs from DOP853 by at most $5.17\times10^{-9}$. These values satisfy the predeclared numerical gates by large margins.

The absence of a turning event is stronger than a finite-time numerical observation for this particular baseline. Its periodic potential has $V_{\min}=3.5\Mpl^4$, so Eq.~\eqref{eq:turning-upper-bound} requires every regular closed-FLRW turning point to obey
\begin{equation}
a_\star\le\sqrt{3/3.5}=0.9258\ldots .
\end{equation}
The baseline instead begins at $a_0=1$ with $H_0>0$. Until a first future turnaround, $a$ can only increase, and therefore it can never re-enter the analytically allowed turning region. The expanding canonical baseline is consequently excluded from every future regular turnaround and hence from future cyclic recurrence, independently of the choice $t_{\rm end}=8\,\Mpl^{-1}$. The machine-readable turning-feasibility certificate records this exclusion separately from numerical convergence and physical-domain termination. This result falsifies recurrence for the configured baseline trajectory; it does not falsify every point in the broader parameter family.

The Stage~1 candidate command evaluates this exact necessary-condition certificate before launching its tighter DOP853 ladder and independent Radau integration. For the canonical baseline the certificate is decisive, so the expensive recurrence verification is short-circuited with the machine-readable status \texttt{analytically\_excluded\_future\_turnaround}, with \texttt{candidate\_gate\_passed=false} and \texttt{stage2\_input\_eligible=false}. Thus the pipeline does not waste additional integration time trying to manufacture recurrence in a trajectory already excluded by the field equations. No Poincar\'e/Floquet stability claim is made for it.

\subsection{Reproduction of the DESI DR2 flat-$\Lambda$CDM posterior}

The official DESI DR2 chains are processed with GetDist after a convergence-driven burn selection, and CAMB supplies the calibrated drag scale, posterior residual predictions, high-redshift background histories, and independent MAP regression. The comparison with the published DESI numbers is generated from the same posterior arrays as the figure.
