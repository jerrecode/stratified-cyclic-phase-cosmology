\section{Computational results}
\subsection{Canonical background: a converged non-cycle}
The configured initial state satisfies the closed-FLRW constraint and selects the expanding branch. Over $0\le t\le8\,\Mpl^{-1}$, the scale factor grows from $1$ to $3.949\times10^3$, $H$ grows from $0.426$ to $1.080$ in natural units, and the scalar relaxes toward the potential minimum. No root-localized bounce or turnaround is detected. The maximum normalized Friedmann residual is $8.75\times10^{-11}$; the stored medium-tolerance solution differs from the reference by at most $4.66\times10^{-8}$, and the RK45 solution differs from DOP853 by at most $5.17\times10^{-9}$. These values satisfy the predeclared numerical gates by large margins. They establish numerical consistency of this trajectory, not physical viability or recurrence.

The releases are not combined. The validation therefore supplies no BAO-only basis for claiming new late-time physics.

\subsection{Reproduction of the DESI DR2 flat-$\Lambda$CDM posterior}
The principal observational result in this paper is deliberately a reproduction benchmark rather than an \SCPC{} constraint. The official DESI DR2 chains are processed with GetDist after a convergence-driven burn selection, and CAMB supplies the calibrated drag scale and independent MAP regression. The comparison with the published DESI numbers is generated from the same posterior arrays as the figure.

The deterministic CAMB MAP regression of the pinned Gaussian BAO likelihood gives $\chi^2_{\rm BAO}=10.28$ for $\nu=11$ and $p_{\rm gof}\simeq0.505$. Agreement between the released-chain reproduction and the published DESI constraints therefore validates the data ingestion, covariance ordering, chain handling, and standard-cosmology backend without promoting those constraints to evidence for \SCPC{}.
