\section{Falsifiability and rejection criteria}
\label{sec:falsifiability}

A proposed \SCPC\ realization is rejected if no nonsingular cyclic solution exists in its declared parameter domain, if recurrence is numerical rather than convergent, if the Friedmann or Hamiltonian constraint fails to converge, if perturbations contain ghosts or gradient instabilities, if the theory lacks a controlled general-relativistic limit, or if every stable parameter region is excluded by the declared likelihood set.

A spectral feature is rejected if its position or significance is unstable under duration, resolution, window, solver, prior, or look-elsewhere corrections. A topological interpretation is rejected unless an invariant quantity distinguishes the claimed sectors or defects.
