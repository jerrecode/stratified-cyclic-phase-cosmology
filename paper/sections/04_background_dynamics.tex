\section{Homogeneous background dynamics}
\label{sec:background}

We use the FLRW line element
\begin{equation}
\dd s^2=-\dd t^2+a^2(t)\gamma_{ij}\dd x^i\dd x^j,
\end{equation}
with spatial curvature $k\in\{-1,0,+1\}$. The background equations are
\begin{align}
3\Mpl^2\left(H^2+\frac{k}{a^2}\right)&=\rho_{\mathrm{tot}},\\
-2\Mpl^2\left(\dot H-\frac{k}{a^2}\right)&=\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}+p_{\mathrm{tot}},\\
\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V_{,\phi}&=0.
\end{align}
A bounce at $t_b$ requires $H(t_b)=0$ and $\dot H(t_b)>0$; a turnaround requires $H(t_t)=0$ and $\dot H(t_t)<0$. Repeated zero crossings are not sufficient to establish a stable limit cycle. A Poincar\'{e} map and Floquet analysis are required for that stronger claim.
