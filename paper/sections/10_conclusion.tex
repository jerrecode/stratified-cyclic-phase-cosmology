\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have defined the clean-room scientific and computational foundation of \SCPC. The framework begins with a covariant action, separates physical and numerical geometries, monitors constraints explicitly, registers authoritative public data releases, and states the conditions under which cyclic, thermodynamic, spectral, or topological claims must be abandoned. The next milestones are parameter-space cycle classification, perturbation equations, stability analysis, and a validated observable interface.
