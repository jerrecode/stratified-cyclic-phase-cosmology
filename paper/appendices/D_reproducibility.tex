\section{Reproducibility and data availability}
\label{app:reproducibility}

The repository contains simulation code, configurations, tests, manuscript sources, and release manifests. Provider-scale chains, maps, catalogs, and strain remain in their authoritative archives unless they are small analysis-critical snapshots whose licenses permit pinning. Every generated inference report records the Git commit, inference-configuration SHA-256 hash, Python/platform information, relevant package versions, external-product URLs, byte sizes, and SHA-256 checksums. The official DESI chain metadata products are retained alongside the sample-file provenance, so convergence and sampling settings can be audited rather than inferred from the figure.

From the repository root,
\begin{verbatim}
python scripts/reproduce.py
python scripts/reproduce_desi_dr2_bbn.py --download-official-chains
latexmk -pdf -cd paper/main.tex
\end{verbatim}
regenerates the canonical background, tolerance verification, standard-comparator diagnostic, exact $S^4$ schematic, fixed-background layered test, DESI chain/convergence products, principal and supplementary inference figures, generated tables, and manuscript. The dedicated GitHub Actions inference workflow executes the same sequence in a clean environment and publishes both the PDF and machine-readable inference products as an artifact.

The analysis-ready DESI Gaussian mean vector and covariance are checksum-pinned to their upstream release commit. The much larger official MCMC chains remain external but are downloaded atomically and hashed at reproduction time. The later 2026 Lyman-$\alpha$ AP+BAO update remains a distinct release product and is not multiplied into the pinned 2025 Results I/II likelihood without an official joint likelihood or covariance. Large raw products are not manuscript dependencies unless the exact archive selection and transformation workflow is recorded.
