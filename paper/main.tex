\begin{abstract}
We introduce Stratified Cyclic Phase Cosmology (\SCPC) as a research framework in which a covariant phase field organizes cosmological states into distinguishable strata and may, for suitable dynamics, support nonsingular sequences of expansion, turnaround, contraction, and bounce. This first paper defines a conservative general-relativistic baseline with a canonical scalar field in a closed Friedmann--Lema\^{i}tre--Robertson--Walker spacetime, establishes its conservation and constraint structure, specifies numerical verification requirements, and defines a data-to-observable architecture for comparison with established non-cyclic and cyclic cosmologies. No stable cycle or empirical detection is assumed. Candidate models are required to satisfy dimensional consistency, controlled standard-cosmology limits, constraint convergence, perturbative stability, reproducibility, and predeclared rejection criteria.
\end{abstract}
